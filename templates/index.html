This project brings SVG conversion, interactive 3D examples, and equation-drawing lessons together in one place. Explore a finished graph or learn how to build one yourself.

01

SVG 2D

Upload an SVG image and convert its paths and shapes into expressions that can be explored in the Desmos graphing calculator.

02

3D Demos

Explore surfaces, intersections, vectors, transformations, coordinate systems, and other ideas in an interactive 3D space.

03

Equation Lab

Practice writing equations from target pictures, reveal progressive hints, check your work, and study a complete solution.

04

Marble Lab

Shape a graph into a track, launch a marble, collect every star, and discover how function parameters change its path.

A simple learning flow

  3. UnderstandConnect each expression to the geometry it creates.
